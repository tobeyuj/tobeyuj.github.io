What you do

  • 7 Cities

    Honolulu to Washington, D.C., each ruled by a deadly sin and the person who feeds it.

  • 252 Landmarks

    Every outdoor screen is a real place. Photograph them all, then post them to grow your followers.

  • 200 Verses

    Real King James scripture, hidden across the cities. Nobody is ever killed: fights end with someone incapacitated or enlightened.

  • 24/7 Real time

    Trips, rest and posts take real time and keep going when you close the app. Waiting is always free.
