Time is the currency

Day 1 8:00 AM

The game's clock started when you opened this page. One real hour is one game day.

The clock keeps running when you close the app. Trips, rest and social posts all take real time, so you can walk away and come back later.

Waiting is always free. If you'd rather not wait, credits skip it: one credit per real minute, or 300 for a trip on to the next city. Everyone starts with 100.

Credits skip waits and nothing else. No items, power or content will ever be for sale.
