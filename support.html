The game says it can't verify the time

The game checks the time online before a wait ends, a post pays out, a skip is bought or an ad pays its reward. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again. Your game is safe, and the clock keeps running in the meantime.

Does the clock really run while the app is closed?

Yes. One real hour is one game day, all the time. Start the 5-hour ocean liner to Los Angeles in the morning, close the app, and you can be there by lunch.

Can I play offline?

Yes. You can walk, talk and fight with no connection. You only need one for the time check above.

Where is my save?

On your device and nowhere else. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so I can't restore a lost game. A full device backup and restore may carry it to a new phone, but I can't promise that.

What do credits do?

They skip waits: one credit per real minute, or 300 for a trip on to the next city. Everyone starts with 100. Credits never buy items or power, and waiting is always free.

What happens to my credits if I start over?

NEW GAME keeps your credits. Deleting the app deletes them, including any you bought, and they can't be brought back.

How do I get a refund?

Credit packs aren't on sale yet. When they are, Apple or Google handles all payments and refunds. Ask at Apple's Report a Problem page or Google Play's refund page.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. That removes everything the game stored. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What does the game run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and 64-bit Android phones and tablets. It plays in landscape.
